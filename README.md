# Workstation CLI

**Workstation CLI** is an offline, terminal-based chemistry calculator suite with local Ollama-powered AI study tools designed for students and educators.

Made by **Workable Projects** (Created by **Dutchh**).

## Features

1. **Molar Mass Calculator**: Supports simple compounds, parentheses, polyatomic groups, and hydrates (`CuSO4·5H2O`).
2. **Gas Laws Calculator**: Solves missing variables for Ideal Gas Law (`PV=nRT`), Boyle's Law, Charles's Law, Gay-Lussac's Law, Combined Gas Law, and Avogadro's Law.
3. **Stoichiometry**: Mass/Moles/Particles/Volume conversions and balanced equation reaction stoich.
4. **Dilution Calculator**: Solves `M1V1 = M2V2`.
5. **Concentration Calculator**: Molarity, Molality, Mass %, Volume %, ppm, ppb.
6. **Limiting Reagent Calculator**: Identifies limiting & excess reagents and theoretical yield.
7. **Percent Yield Calculator**: `(Actual / Theoretical) * 100`.
8. **Chemistry Reference**: Offline database for Periodic Table, Polyatomic Ions, Solubility Rules, Strong Acids & Bases, SI Prefixes, and Constants.
9. **AI Tools**: Local Ollama-backed AI Chat, Quiz Generator, Study Planner, Essay Helper, and Socratic Tutor.
10. **CLI Information**: Version and author credits.
